## Role: Team Leader
Led the university team in the ASC26 Student Supercomputer Challenge, managing the design and optimization of a high-performance computing cluster.

## Key Achievements
* **Cluster Design:** Configured a high-performance cluster system within a 3000W power constraint.
* **Performance Tuning:** Conducted benchmarking and optimization for **HPL** (High-Performance Linpack) and **HPCG**.
* **Environment Management:** Managed software stacks using **Linux**, **Slurm**, and **Intel OneAPI** (MPI, MKL).

## Technical Environment
* HPC Architecture, Parallel Computing, Performance Profiling.

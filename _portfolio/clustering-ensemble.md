## Abstract
This research introduces an optimization approach for clustering ensembles within adaptive multiplex networks. By leveraging diffusion theory and generalization principles, the method improves clustering stability and accuracy in high-dimensional data environments.

## Key Contributions
* **Adaptive Weighting:** Developed a mechanism to assign weights in multiplex networks dynamically.
* **Manifold Optimization:** Integrated manifold learning to refine the diffusion process.
* **Algorithm Comparison:** Demonstrated superior performance over CEAM and LWGP algorithms on the YaleB dataset.
